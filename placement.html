Training & Placement Cell: Objectives

  • To maintain good liaison with industries and corporate houses.
  • To arrange campus interviews for final year students.
  • To organize industrial training for students during vacations.
  • To promote and encourage students for sponsored projects.
  • To arrange expert lectures and guidance programs by industry experts.
  • To maintain data banks of placed alumni and provide placement opportunities.
  • To organize industrial visits for practical exposure and knowledge.
