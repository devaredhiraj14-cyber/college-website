Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust (SSBT)

A philanthropic educational trust dedicated to empowering rural and semi-urban youth through high-caliber academic programmes.

Shram Sadhana Bombay Trust was registered in 1983 with the noble intention of spreading the light of knowledge to every stratum of society. Guided by the values of dedication, humanism, and nation-building, the trust runs prestigious technical and degree colleges at Bambhori, Jalgaon.

SSBT's Arts, Commerce & Science College offers forward-looking degree programmes including BCA, BBA, B.Sc. Computer Science, B.Com, and B.A., affiliated with Kavayitri Bahinabai Chaudhari North Maharashtra University (KBCNMU), Jalgaon and accredited with Grade 'B' by NAAC.

Campus Facilities designed

Computer Labs

Central Knowledge Center

Expansive library stocked with over 15,000 curriculum textbooks, national and international journals, DELNET e-resources, and a peaceful reading hall.
