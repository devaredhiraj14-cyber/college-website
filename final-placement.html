Committed to nurturing industry-ready graduates.

The Training & Placement Cell serves as the institutional interface between corporate recruiters and students from BCA, BBA, B.Sc. Computer Science, Commerce, and Arts.

Our philosophy is grounded in comprehensive, continuous training. From the beginning of second year, students undergo systematic grooming in communicative English, algorithmic problem solving, quantitative aptitude, mock interviews, and group discussions.

The cell actively liaises with IT corporations, banking institutions, manufacturing units, and startups to facilitate on-campus, pooled, and off-campus recruitment drives throughout the academic calendar.
