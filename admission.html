GOVERNMENT FINANCIAL SCHEMES

Scholarships & Fee Concessions.

Our dedicated scholarship cell assists every eligible student in applying for Government of Maharashtra MahaDBT schemes.

Post-Matric Scholarships

100% tuition and examination fee waiver for eligible SC/ST category students, and 50% waiver for VJNT/SBC/OBC students as per MahaDBT regulations.

Rajarshi Chhatrapati Shahu Maharaj EBC Scheme

50% tuition and exam fee concession for economically backward general category students whose annual family income is below ₹8 Lakhs.

Minority Scholarships

Government of Maharashtra financial assistance for meritorious students belonging to Muslim, Christian, Buddhist, Sikh, Parsi, and Jain communities.
