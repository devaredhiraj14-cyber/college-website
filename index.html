CAMPUS ADVANTAGES

Why study at SSBT College?

Our commitment to student welfare, technical competence, and career readiness distinguishes us as a preferred college in the Khandesh region.

Hi-Tech Computer Labs

Air-conditioned computer laboratories with high-speed optical fiber internet, modern development tools and server infrastructure.

Comprehensive Library

Thousands of reference books, national/international journals, e-library subscriptions and spacious reading halls.

Experienced Faculty

Qualified professors, Ph.D. holders, and dedicated mentors committed to student academic success and personal mentoring.

Dedicated Placement Cell

Proactive training in soft skills, quantitative aptitude, mock interviews, and on-campus placement drives with top recruiters.

Sports & NSS Units

Large sports ground, indoor games, active NSS community outreach, and cultural clubs fostering well-rounded personality development.

MahaDBT Scholarships

Full support for government scholarship schemes, EBC fee concessions, and minority financial aid benefits.
