TRAVEL CONVEYANCE

How to Reach Our Campus.

Accessibility from central transportation hubs in Jalgaon city.

By Railway

Jalgaon Junction is a major railway hub on the Central Railway network. The college campus is located 6 km from the station, easily reachable by auto-rickshaw or college bus.

By Bus / Road

The campus is situated right on National Highway 6 (NH-6). State Transport (MSRTC) buses and city buses stop directly in front of the college main gate at Bambhori.

By Air

Jalgaon Airport is located approximately 14 km away with domestic connectivity. Alternatively, Aurangabad (Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar) Airport is situated 160 km away.
