Department Overview, Vision & Objectives

Cultivating technical competence, software craftsmanship, and ethical IT leadership.

Department Vision

To emerge as a premier center of computer education and technological innovation, producing globally competent software professionals, innovators, and entrepreneurs capable of driving digital solutions.

Department Mission

To provide high-quality technical education through modern curricula, hands-on programming labs, industrial internships, and ethical training that meets the evolving demands of the global software industry.

Program Objectives (PEOs)

Graduates will master core computer science foundations, exhibit proficiency in modern programming stacks (Java, Python, MERN), and demonstrate high professional ethics, teamwork, and leadership.
